TRY AN INTERACTION

Start the bubble. Allow simulated access, show it, then tap for the shelf or hold for search.

Move or dismiss. Drag the bubble to an edge, or onto the bottom X to hide it.

Save and share. Add a note, select a card, move it, share it, or confirm deletion.

Circle a detail. Approve sample capture, draw around the text, then Copy, Stash, Search, Lens, or Share.

Try the gesture strip. With the bubble on, swipe upward from the bottom strip; enable hold or double tap in Settings.

Make a card stack. Select cards in the floating shelf, then drag the stack upward into the sample app.
