# StashDrop 0.8.0

A new light look inspired by vivo OriginOS: soft grey ground, big white rounded cards, bold Outfit titles and one accent colour you choose.

## Changes

- **Home**: a Circle to Search card with a "Try it now" button, an edge shelf on/off tile, a shelf count tile, the clipboard switch and your three most recent items. The pill navigation shows the active tab's name.
- **Shelf**: search your saved items as you type. Collection and type chips are ink when selected. Photos and videos sit two per row; notes and links get full-width cards with their collection pill. Selected cards tilt slightly with an accent border, and Share, WhatsApp, Move and Delete live in a dark floating bar.
- **Settings**: a setup ring shows how many of edge shelf, notifications and Google readiness are done, with a one-tap Fix. Permissions, Circle to Search and digital assistant options are grouped in white cards.
- **Your way** (Settings → Gestures, clipboard & feel): gesture strip, clipboard, haptics, springiness and the new **accent colour** choice — Blue, Sky, Violet or Orange.
- **Edge shelf overlay**: the handle is an accent pill with the shelf icon. The panel, cards, swipe-to-delete background and the chat-head dismiss X use the same light style and follow your accent.
- Fixes: cached previews no longer flash a placeholder on first layout, and content can no longer scroll behind the bottom bars.

## Update

Install `StashDrop-v0.8.0.apk` over the official app. Version code **11**, package `com.vibe.stashdrop`, Room schema and the release signing certificate are unchanged, so saved items stay available.

The Outfit typeface is bundled under the SIL Open Font License; its licence text ships in the app at `assets/licenses/outfit-ofl.txt`.

## Verification and limits

Automated unit, interaction and Paparazzi render tests cover the new screens. Gallery images render the production Compose components with sample data; they are not phone screenshots. Physical-device checks (overlay feel, accent on the live overlay, Google Circle to Search eligibility) remain items for the [device checklist](https://github.com/ankitchanki/StashDrop/blob/main/docs/DEVICE_VALIDATION.md).
